01 / About

Fashion should
feel like you.

Fashion, but make it personal.

Ariska creates content around the way she naturally sees fashion: modest, feminine, effortless and always with a little personality.

Her style leans toward syar'i and modest fashion, while staying open to modern silhouettes, beauty and contemporary trends. Whether it is a full look, a simple styling detail or a beauty moment, she turns everyday inspiration into content that feels authentic, approachable and visually engaging.
